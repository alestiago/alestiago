Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the Padel Coach web app (the "App") hosted at alestiago.com handles personal information.

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No accounts, logins, forms, analytics, or third-party trackers are used by the App. All functionality runs client-side in your device.

Because the App does not collect personal data, there is no data processing, sharing, or storage beyond what occurs locally in your device during a session.

Third-party content

The App may include links to other websites. This Privacy Policy applies only to content provided directly by the App. Please review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will indicate when changes were made.
